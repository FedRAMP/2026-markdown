# 20x Deadlines

This is a top level overview of deadlines that apply within each specific ruleset for providers.

**_Optional Adoption Allowed_** - Providers may adopt this ruleset in its entirety after this date for both initial and ongoing FedRAMP Certification.

**_Obtaining Initial Certification_** - If a provider applies for initial FedRAMP Certification after this date, the ruleset MUST be adopted or FedRAMP Certification will be denied.

**_Maintaining Ongoing Certification_** - Providers with an existing FedRAMP Certification SHOULD adopt the ruleset by this date; if it is not adopted by this data, a corrective action plan to adopt the ruleset by the end of the grace period will be required to maintain FedRAMP Certification.

**_Grace Period Ends_** - Providers with an existing FedRAMP Certification MUST adopt the ruleset by this date or FedRAMP Certification will be revoked.
